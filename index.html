01 — About

Deeper than
any mountain

The Aye Maties Pirate Challenge is a movement born from the ocean. We gather young pirates from around the world — not to plunder, but to protect. Every dive, every cleanup, every challenge brings us closer to the sea we want to keep.

02 — Mission

We don't wait.
We dive in.

Our mission is simple: make ocean conservation tangible, exciting, and accessible to every young person on the planet. Through real challenges, real cleanups, and real community — we turn awareness into action.

Every reef matters. Every action counts.

03 — Goals

What we're
fighting for.

  • 01
    Ocean Cleanup Organise regular coastal and underwater cleanup events with youth teams worldwide.
  • 02
    Marine Education Bring ocean science and conservation into schools through interactive programmes.
  • 03
    Coral Restoration Partner with marine biologists to grow and replant coral in damaged reef systems.
  • 04
    Youth Leadership Empower the next generation to lead the global conversation on ocean health.
