The Format

About Aye Maties

Aye Maties is an internationally adaptable, partly scripted reality format that brings a diverse group of participants aboard a large working sailing ship to take on authentic maritime, historical and ecological challenges.

The confined, constantly moving environment leaves no room for delay and demands swift cooperation and decisive action under real pressure. Conflicts are not staged; they arise naturally from spatial conditions, environmental demands and interpersonal dynamics. The format deliberately structures this tension — centred on dialogues between generations: Care vs. Ignorance, Duty vs. Temptation and Tradition vs. modern superficiality — to drive personal growth and narrative progression.
