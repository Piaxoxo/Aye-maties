The Format

What is Aye Maties?

Aye Maties — Pirate Challenge is a new cinematic TV and entertainment adventure built around the power of the sea, the thrill of discovery and the strength of a crew facing extraordinary challenges together.

Guided by the legendary Cpt. Old Salt, modern-day challengers enter a world of maritime trials, hidden mysteries, teamwork and unforgettable ocean-bound experiences. But behind every adventure lies a greater purpose: drawing attention to the beauty, fragility and protection of our oceans.

Combining powerful storytelling, an international entertainment concept and authentic maritime atmosphere, Aye Maties invites audiences to follow an adventure where the greatest treasure is the future of our seas.

The Crew

Meet the Faces of Aye Maties

A crew of storytellers, artists and adventurers — each bringing their craft and spirit to the world of Aye Maties.

OS

Cpt. Old Salt

Creator · Executive Producer

The heart and original creator of Aye Maties. A highly experienced USCG 500-Ton Captain, he brings authentic maritime expertise, leadership and the true spirit of adventure. As Cpt. Old Salt he leads the crew into a world of mystery and ocean-bound discovery — the visionary who first set the course, and continues to guide its journey from idea to reality.

PS

Pia Alice Sekulic

Head of Marketing

Pia leads the marketing of Aye Maties, bringing strategy, creativity and a deep understanding of audience behaviour. With a background in Neuromarketing and Business Psychology studied in Vienna, she turns stories into meaningful connections and builds an engaged community. As Head of Marketing, she ensures the spirit and mission of Aye Maties reach audiences far beyond the horizon.

DS

Dino Stelzl

Film Director · Producer

The film director and producer behind the cinematic world of Aye Maties. With years of experience in television, film production and visual storytelling, he transforms the adventurous vision into powerful images and compelling stories — combining classic filmmaking with modern creative technologies to shape the distinctive look and narrative spirit of the project.

AN

Anita “Niddl” Stelzl

Presenter · Singer · Sea Soul

The multi-faceted ‘Niddl’ joins Aye Maties as a powerful singer, charismatic presenter and true soul of the sea. Known from her success on Starmania and her vibrant career in music and entertainment, she brings unique energy and voice to the project — a passionate advocate for the ocean who helps share the mission of protecting our seas.

NZ

Natascha Zenig

Makeup · SFX · Body Painter

Natascha brings the characters and visual world of Aye Maties to life through makeup, special effects and body art. Based between Vienna, Los Angeles and Cancún, she combines professional artistry with a passion for expressive Dark Art — character makeup, SFX transformations and detailed body painting that turn the crew into authentic figures of adventure, mystery and the sea.

SP

Silas Pross

Director of Photography · CGI

Silas shapes the visual world of Aye Maties through cinematography, camera work and digital creativity. As Director of Photography he captures the cinematic atmosphere — from powerful character moments to dramatic adventures at sea — while his expertise in CGI extends the world beyond the camera. His technical precision and artistic eye bring the voyage vividly to the screen.

MS

Michael Schumpelt

Cameraman · Visual AI Expert

Michael combines the art of cinematography with the creative possibilities of artificial intelligence. As a cameraman he captures the atmosphere, energy and authentic moments of the adventure, while his expertise in Visual AI helps develop innovative imagery and visual concepts that expand the cinematic world of Aye Maties.

ML

Michael Lehner

Set Photographer

Michael captures the world of Aye Maties beyond the moving image. As Set Photographer he documents the atmosphere, characters and unforgettable moments behind the adventure — creating powerful stills that preserve the spirit of the production for promotion, storytelling and lasting memories of the voyage.

MH

Matthias Hauser

Set Hand · First Assistant · Driver

A reliable and versatile member of the production crew. As Set Hand and First Assistant, Matthias supports the team wherever practical coordination and hands-on assistance are needed. As Driver he ensures crew, equipment and production reach their destination safely and on time — keeping the adventure moving, on set and on the road.

KS

Koray Schober

Second Assistant · Driver

Koray supports the production team as Second Assistant and Driver. With reliability, flexibility and a hands-on approach, he helps ensure that people, equipment and essential production needs are in the right place at the right time — playing an important role in keeping the voyage organised, efficient and moving forward.
