This Privacy Policy explains how Sail Wisely (Ireland) CLG (“Aye Maties”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data in connection with this website, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

Sail Wisely (Ireland) CLG · c/o Capt. Peter Brucha, Jubiläumstrasse 19, 2345 Brunn am Gebirge, Austria · AyeMaties@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

This is a largely static website. We do not run our own accounts, newsletters or tracking analytics. Data may be processed in the following limited cases:

  • Server logs: our hosting provider may temporarily process technical data (IP address, browser type, time of request) to deliver the site securely. This is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Local preferences: your soundtrack choice and volume are stored in your browser’s localStorage on your device. This never leaves your device and is not personal data we receive.
  • Email contact: if you write to us, we process your message and contact details solely to respond (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR).

3. Third-party services & embeds

To deliver the cinematic experience, the site loads a small number of external resources. When these load, the respective provider may receive your IP address and set their own cookies:

  • SoundCloud (soundtrack player) — SoundCloud privacy. The player only loads / plays after you actively start it.
  • Google Fonts (typography) — Google privacy.
  • jsDelivr CDN (3D/WebGL library).
  • Outbound links to YouTube and SoundCloud open on their own platforms under their policies.

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Austria: the Datenschutzbehörde). To exercise any right, contact AyeMaties@gmail.com.

5. Retention

We keep email correspondence only as long as necessary to handle your request and to meet legal obligations. Local preferences remain until you clear your browser storage.

Last updated: June 2026. This notice may be updated as the project and its services evolve.

We keep cookies to a strict minimum. This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies of its own.

1. Strictly necessary / preferences

We use your browser’s localStorage (not a tracking cookie) to remember whether you turned the soundtrack on and at what volume, so the experience behaves the way you left it. This stays on your device and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

2. Third-party cookies

  • SoundCloud: when you start the soundtrack, SoundCloud’s embedded player may set cookies needed for playback. See the SoundCloud cookie policy.
  • Google Fonts / jsDelivr: requested to render fonts and the 3D library; these may log technical request data but are not used by us for tracking.

3. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and clear local storage in your browser settings at any time. Doing so may reset your soundtrack preference and limit the embedded player.
